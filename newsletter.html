Session Directors Message

Welcome to FSY. Let’s prepare to Rejoice in Christ together.

To our awesome youth!

We are so excited for the opportunity to share the FSY experience with you. We simply can’t wait to welcome, meet and grow with you.

Our week together will be filled with fun, laughter and experiences designed to strengthen your faith in Jesus Christ.

Today, we wanted to introduce you to some of our Young Single Adult (YSA) leaders who will be running three of the events that happen at FSY. Over the next few weeks, we'll introduce you to the other leaders that you will meet out there.
 

Musical Fireside

Let Your Voice Be Heard!

From Karen & Airi

Hi, we’re Karen and Airi from the Macarthur and Canberra Stakes, and we’re putting together the musical fireside — one of the most spiritual nights of the whole week. Whether you sing solo, in a group or have only ever sung in the shower, we’d love to hear from you. You don’t need to be a professional, just willing to share your testimony through music. Sign up below and we’ll be in touch with rehearsal details.

Variety Show

FSY Sydney’s Got Talent!

From Aleira & Ezekiel

Hi, we’re Aleira and Ezekiel from the Mortdale and Hebersham Stakes, and we’re on the hunt for the hidden talent of FSY Sydney. Singing, dancing, a haka or siva, comedy, an instrument, a magic trick — if it brings joy, we want it on stage. Solo acts and groups are welcome, and every act will get support to shine. Don’t be shy: this is your moment!

Dances

Get Ready to Hit the Dance Floor!

From Jemima & Kelly

Hi, we’re Jemima and Kelly from the Bathurst and Newcastle District and Stake, and we’re planning the two themed dances you won’t want to miss. Start thinking about your outfits now — dressing up is half the fun! Please keep everything modest and in line with the FSY dress standards, and get creative with colour, accessories and a whole lot of attitude.

Back to the 90s

Bright colours, denim, scrunchies and your best retro moves from when the prince was fresh.

Surf’s Up
