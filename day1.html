Week 2: Junior Session

Day 1 Highlights

• Check In + Orientation
• Meet Your Counsellor + Company
• Home Evening Lesson + Games
 
 

Check In + Orientation

What an incredible start to FSY! Today, hundreds of excited (and perhaps slightly nervous!) youth arrived at St Joseph’s, Hunters Hill, ready for a week they’ll never forget. Our amazing counsellors and volunteers were there to greet everyone with big smiles, helping them check in, settle into their accommodation and meet their groups. There were plenty of introductions, a few nervous laughs and the beginnings of what we hope will become lifelong friendships.

Once everyone was settled, it was time to get familiar with their new home for the week! Our orientation activities helped the youth explore the campus, learn what to expect and discover all the exciting experiences ahead. By the end of orientation, unfamiliar faces were becoming friends, nerves were turning into excitement and FSY was officially underway!
 

Meet Your Counsellor + Company

One of the best parts of FSY is discovering that you’re surrounded by people who are about to become some of your closest friends! Today, our youth met their companies and the incredible counsellors who will be guiding, encouraging and cheering them on throughout the week. Through introductions, games and plenty of laughter, everyone began getting to know the people they’ll be sharing this adventure with.

Our counsellors have been preparing for this moment, and it was wonderful to see their enthusiasm come to life. Whether it was helping someone feel included, remembering a new name or encouraging a quieter group member to join in, the focus was on making sure every young person felt they belonged. And judging by the smiles, we’re off to a fantastic start!
 

Home Evening Lesson & Games

Tonight, our youth gathered with their companies for a special Family Home Evening, bringing the first day of FSY back to what matters most: our faith in Jesus Christ. Through uplifting messages, meaningful discussions and shared experiences, everyone had an opportunity to reflect on why they’re here and what they hope to gain from this week.

One of the beautiful things about FSY is that spiritual experiences often begin with something simple: a thoughtful question, a sincere testimony or the courage to share how you’re feeling. Tonight was an opportunity to create that foundation. Our hope is that every young person begins to recognise that FSY isn’t just about the activities and friendships, but about discovering how much their Saviour loves them.
 

From the Session Directors

Ideas for conversations to have with the youth about what we did today

One of the purposes of FSY is to empower the youth to continue having spiritual experiences at home. As parents, you have the ability to bring conversations to them that will help them maintain their connection to the Saviour and His gospel beyond their week with us. This is a sacred privilege and responsibility.

Each day we’ll suggest some ideas of questions that you could ask them either now (if they have a phone at FSY and are checking in with you) or when they return home.

• What were you nervous about today, and how did you find the courage to overcome it?
• What did working together teach you about unity and helping others feel included?
• When did you step outside your comfort zone, and what did it teach you about yourself?
• What did you learn about someone today that helped you see them as Heavenly Father might?
• What’s one thing you experienced today that you’d love to make part of your life at home?

One last thing 🤗

We want you to know that for some of the youth, the early days of FSY can be daunting, overwhelming and make them feel a little homesick. In the lead-up to this conference, our wellness team have spent time specifically planning for this.

By day three, these fears are usually lowered. We appreciate your support and prayers for our youth in these early days. We know they are heard.
